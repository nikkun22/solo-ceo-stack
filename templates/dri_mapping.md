# DRI Mapping Template / DRI（直接責任者）マッピングテンプレート

> **DRI = Directly Responsible Individual**
> One CxO owns each business line end-to-end. "Owns" means accountable for outcomes,
> not that they do all the work. Replace placeholders with your actual business lines.

---

## How to Use / 使い方

1. Replace `[Business Line N]` with your actual product, service, or revenue stream name.
2. Assign the Primary DRI — the CxO who is accountable for that line's performance.
3. Assign Supporting CxOs — those consulted regularly (not accountable, but essential contributors).
4. Set review cadence and KPIs.
5. Review this mapping every quarter or when the business model changes.

## Review Cadence Reference / レビュータイミング設定例

### Monthly Review (月次レビュー)

- **Trigger**: First business day of each month
- **Who**: DRI + CFO + CEO
- **Agenda**:
  1. Actuals vs. targets for the prior month
  2. Top 3 issues or blockers
  3. Forecast update for next 3 months
- **Output**: Updated metric dashboard; any escalations to president

### Quarterly Strategic Review (四半期戦略レビュー)

- **Trigger**: End of Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4
- **Who**: All CxOs + President
- **Agenda**:
  1. Full P&L and KPI review per business line
  2. DRI accountability assessment
  3. Roadmap adjustments for next quarter
  4. DRI reassignment if needed
- **Output**: Revised DRI mapping table; updated OKRs; CEO decision log entry

### Milestone-Based Review (マイルストーン到達時レビュー)

Define milestones in advance. Examples:

| Milestone | Trigger | Action |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Revenue milestone | MRR reaches ¥[X] | Full P&L review + resource allocation meeting |
| Launch milestone | New product/feature goes live | 30-day post-launch review |
| Risk milestone | Churn exceeds X% | Emergency review with CSO + CEO within 48h |
| Partnership milestone | Contract signed | 90-day partnership health check |
| Cost milestone | Burn rate exceeds budget by 20% | CFO + CEO emergency review |

## DRI Escalation Rules / エスカレーションルール

```
If DRI cannot resolve a blocker within their authority:
  → Escalate to CEO within 24 hours (not at the next scheduled review)

If two DRIs have conflicting resource needs:
  → Escalate to COO for mediation
  → COO escalates to CEO if unresolved within 48 hours

If a business line KPI misses target by >30% in two consecutive months:
  → Mandatory strategic review regardless of quarterly schedule
  → President informed immediately

If DRI role needs to change:
  → CHRO drafts the role transition plan
  → CEO approves
  → COO executes the handover
```
